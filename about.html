About

About Dr. Kerry B. Lewis

Entrepreneur, publisher, and author — a Southerner by birth who built his life and his work in Los Angeles, and who believes nobody is beyond a second chance, because he needed one himself, and got one.

From Griffin to Los Angeles

Dr. Kerry B. Lewis was born on June 5, 1991, in Griffin, Georgia, and raised in Daytona Beach, Florida. Before business, there was ministry: from 2011 to 2013 he served as a youth pastor in Savannah, Georgia — work that put him face to face with families living on the edge, and shaped everything that came after.

He went on to earn a Master’s in Business and Finance and an Honorary Doctorate in Divinity, and attended the University of Phoenix. He also knows the other side of the American economy firsthand: he has been homeless himself. It is the experience he cites most — the reason he says he doesn’t need a briefing book to understand the streets.

Today he lives in Los Angeles, where he runs businesses, publishes news, and writes fiction.

A note to you, from Kerry

If you only know the headlines, you don't know me yet.

I grew up in the South, I served young people in ministry, I built businesses with my own hands, and I have also had seasons where I had almost nothing at all. Every one of those chapters taught me the same thing: people are more than their worst day, and a city is more than its worst street.

I write because stories are how we remember that. I build because a job, a home, and a second chance are worth more than a speech about them. And I put my name on all of it, plainly, because I am not asking anyone to trust a brand. I am asking you to know a person.

— Dr. Kerry B. Lewis
