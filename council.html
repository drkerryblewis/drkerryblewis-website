Selma Avenue Elementary School Project

A community program proposal by The Treasure Grid Inc. — after-school and low-income family support in Hollywood, in a joint-use partnership with LAUSD.

Status — Early Talks Only

Proposal sent to LAUSD on October 28, 2026. LAUSD's Facilities Development Manager replied on October 29, 2026, offering to schedule a discussion. No agreement has been reached, no program dates have been set, and nothing has been promised about the campus. Everything on this page is a proposal and a concept for discussion, not a promise.

Selma Avenue Elementary School, 6611 Selma Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90028 — a low, single-level front building with a gated entrance on Selma Ave. Photo: Dr. Kerry B. Lewis.

The Proposal, In Plain Terms

The Treasure Grid Inc. proposes a joint-use partnership with LAUSD. LAUSD keeps the campus and controls it. The Treasure Grid would run a supervised, structured after-school place for young people — homework help, learning, activities that build confidence and life skills, and a safe space until families can pick them up — alongside support for low-income families, including help connecting to food, housing, benefits, and other community services.

This is a community program, not a replacement school and not a shelter. No overnight use is requested.

  • School: Selma Avenue Elementary School, 6611 Selma Ave, Los Angeles, CA 90028, Hollywood — LAUSD, grades K–5, founded 1910.
  • Campus size (public records): about 1.115 acres (roughly 48,500 square feet of land) for the main parcel — a small, compact neighborhood campus. Interior square footage, classroom count, and condition are not in public listings and must be confirmed by LAUSD and a site walk-through.
  • Proposed hours: Monday–Friday, 7:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m.; Saturday–Sunday, 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. No overnight use.
  • Evening note: Kids Night Out and Teens Night would need extended evening hours agreed separately with LAUSD — they are not covered by the proposed daytime window unless LAUSD agrees.
  • Funding: self-funded by The Treasure Grid Inc.; specific funding details to be discussed during the agreement process. No budget figure is claimed.
  • Still to be agreed: staffing, insurance, safety and supervision details, start date, and which rooms are included. None are set.
  • About The Treasure Grid Inc.: California nonprofit corporation, Entity No. 4745656, filed May 19, 2021, active with the California Secretary of State. No claim of 501(c)(3) status is made here.

Layout of the Building — Concept Only

This layout is a concept for discussion only — not an architectural plan, not to scale, and not agreed by LAUSD. It uses the kinds of spaces a small elementary campus normally has. If a room does not exist, or LAUSD keeps it, that zone moves or drops. The program does not expand into space it was not given. A separate nearby parking parcel (1626 Cherokee Ave) is not counted as part of the campus unless LAUSD says so.

Zone 1 — Front Entrance & Welcome (Selma Ave gate)
Secure check-in for youth and families, sign-in/sign-out for pickup, family welcome desk. Visitors check in here; they do not wander the campus.
Zone 7 — Parking & Drop-Off
Orderly family pickup and drop-off at the front, staffed, children released only to their families. Parking only as LAUSD allows.
Zone 2 — Classrooms Wing
After School: homework help, reading, tutoring, confidence and life-skills activities. Classes and small groups in classroom-sized groups.
Zone 3 — Multipurpose Room / Cafeteria
Hot Meals (kitchen use only if LAUSD allows, otherwise approved setup in the room), Kids Night Out, Teens Night, family events and group meetings.
Zone 4 — Library / Media Room: Help Desk
Quiet study and reading. One-on-one benefits, IDs, housing, and case-management help, with privacy for personal paperwork. Computer and phone access.
Zone 6 — Support Rooms
Mail, phone, and charging station (supervised); locked move-in-kit and food storage; staff and volunteer base.
Zone 5 — Outdoor Play Area & Blacktop
Supervised outdoor play and recreation for youth programs, and open-air community time on weekends, inside the campus boundary and within agreed hours. No program spills onto the street.

Layout key: Zones 1 and 7 are the front of the campus (Selma Ave side). Zones 2, 3, 4, and 6 are the indoor spaces. Zone 5 is the outdoor play area. This is a concept diagram in words, not a measured floor plan.

A Day, In Concept (Within Agreed Hours Only)

  • Weekday afternoons: After School check-in → homework and classrooms → outdoor play → Hot Meals in the multipurpose room → family pickup.
  • Weekends: Family resource help in the library/help desk, groups and classes in classrooms, community time outdoors.
  • Evenings (only if LAUSD agrees extended hours): Kids Night Out and Teens Night in the multipurpose room, with the same check-in and pickup rules.

What This Project Does NOT Do

  • It is not a school and not a shelter. No overnight use, no sleeping quarters, no one lives on campus.
  • It does not take over the whole campus. LAUSD keeps the campus and controls which rooms, hours, and areas are in any agreement.
  • It does not promise any room, date, or program until LAUSD agrees in writing.
