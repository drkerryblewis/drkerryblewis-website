TOP STORY

Working With LAUSD — Selma Avenue Elementary School

October 29, 2026.

The Treasure Grid Inc. is in early talks with the Los Angeles Unified School District about programs that could help our community at Selma Avenue Elementary School in Hollywood — a supervised after-school program and support for low-income families, on a joint-use basis, with the campus remaining an LAUSD campus.

We sent our proposal on October 28, 2026, and on October 29, 2026, LAUSD's Facilities Development Manager replied and offered to schedule a time to discuss the program and potential options for use of the campus. That is where the conversation stands today.

We are just at the beginning of the process. No agreement has been reached, no program dates have been set, and nothing has been promised about the campus. More information is to come as the conversation continues, and it will be shared here first.

Meet Kerry

Lived it. Built from it.

Kerry Lewis came up the hard way — born in Griffin, Georgia, raised in Daytona Beach, Florida, and at one point homeless himself. He went on to earn a master’s degree and build businesses from nothing.

That combination — lived experience and a builder’s record — is what he brings to the problems Los Angeles talks about every day.
