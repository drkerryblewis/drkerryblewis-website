Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is — and is not — collected when you visit drkerryblewis.org (the “Site”), the personal website of Dr. Kerry B. Lewis of Los Angeles, California. It also explains the limited ways information you choose to send us is used.

The short version: This Site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no sales, and no advertising trackers. It does not set cookies. We do not sell personal information. The only personal information we ever receive is what you choose to send us yourself — for example, an email.

1. Information you choose to send us

Email. If you contact us at info@drkerryblewis.org or any other address published on this Site, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use that information only to read and respond to you, and to keep a record of the correspondence. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it for marketing.

“Your Voice” comment board. The comment board on this Site runs entirely in your own web browser. On the current version of the Site, comments you type are displayed in your browser for your visit only — they are not transmitted to us, not stored on any server of ours, and they disappear when you leave the page. Please do not post private personal information in a comment.

2. Information collected automatically by service providers

This Site is a static website hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). When you visit, GitHub’s servers may automatically record standard technical logs — such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — for security and operational purposes. That processing is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The Site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. To display those fonts, your browser contacts Google’s servers, which means Google may receive your IP address as part of that request. That processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not run our own analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking tools on this Site, and the Site itself does not set cookies.

3. Links to other websites

This Site links to outside services — for example Facebook, Linktree, and news organizations. When you follow those links, you leave this Site, and the privacy policy of the site you land on applies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

4. How we use and share information

  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To operate, secure, and improve this Site.
  • To comply with the law, or to protect our rights, when legally required.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing or for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Children’s privacy

This Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

6. California residents

Because we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in California law, there is no “Do Not Sell or Share” link to maintain. If you are a California resident and would like to ask what information of yours we hold — which, in practice, would be emails you have sent us — or ask us to delete it, contact us at the address below and we will respond.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
